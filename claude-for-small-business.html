Claude for Small Business: what it actually does

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 7, 2026

Claude for Small Business, launched by Anthropic on May 13, 2026, is a toggle inside Claude that connects it to tools you already run your business on — QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 — with 15 ready-to-run workflows for the tasks owners consistently put off: payroll planning, month-end close, invoice chasing, lead triage, contract review, cash-flow monitoring. There's no separate fee for it; it runs on whatever Claude plan you already pay for.

Worth saying plainly before the details: this is a different topic from almost everything else on this site. Most of what we write about is whether AI can find and recommend your business to customers. This is about whether you use AI to run the business itself — a real, separate question, and this page answers that one honestly rather than stretching it into something it isn't.

What it costs

Nothing beyond your existing Claude subscription. Pro runs $20/month, Max runs $100-200/month, Team runs $25-30 per seat — the small-business connectors and workflows are a toggle inside those plans, not an added line item. You'll still pay for whatever third-party tools you connect it to, same as you already do.

FAQ

What is Claude for Small Business?

A package of connectors and ready-to-run workflows, launched by Anthropic on May 13, 2026, that puts Claude to work inside tools small business owners already use — QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365. It ships with 15 agentic workflows built around tasks owners consistently find tedious: payroll planning, month-end close, invoice chasing, lead triage, contract review, campaign creation, cash-flow monitoring.

How much does Claude for Small Business cost?

No separate fee. It's a toggle inside an existing Claude plan — Pro at $20/month, Max at $100-200/month, or Team at $25-30 per seat — not a new pricing tier. You pay for your Claude subscription and whatever third-party tools you already use, like QuickBooks or PayPal; there's no additional charge layered on top for the small-business connectors themselves.

Does Claude for Small Business run tasks automatically, without approval?

No. Every task or workflow starts because you asked for it. You approve the plan before it runs, or let it run end-to-end once you're comfortable — but nothing executes without you initiating it. Anthropic also states it doesn't train on your data by default on Team and Enterprise plans.

Is this the same as making my business visible to AI search engines?

No, and it's worth being direct about that: Claude for Small Business is a tool you use to run your operations. It has nothing to do with whether ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity can find and recommend your business to customers — that's a separate question about your website and public listings, not about which AI tool you use internally.
