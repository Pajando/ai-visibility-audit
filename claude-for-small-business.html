Chasing invoices and leads after closing? What Claude for Small Business actually does

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 7, 2026

If your nights go to invoices, follow-ups and month-end books, Anthropic built a tool aimed at that. Claude for Small Business launched May 13, 2026. Anthropic calls it "a toggle install that puts Claude to work inside the tools small business owners already use" (Anthropic). The launch post named Intuit QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, and said it "ships with 15 ready-to-run agentic workflows." The product page now also lists Shopify, Stripe and Gusto "and 25 more" (Claude).

A quick note before the details: this is a different topic from most of this site. Most pages here are about whether customers can find your business when they ask AI. This one is about using AI to run the business. Both are real questions. This page answers the second one.

What the workflows actually cover

AreaExample
FinancePlanning payroll, closing the month, margin analysis, tax-season organizing
Sales & collectionsInvoice chasing, lead triage
Operations & marketingContract review, running a campaign, content strategy

These are examples from Anthropic's launch post, which doesn't list all 15. On control, Anthropic's two pages say slightly different things. The launch post says "Every task and workflow you run within Claude is initiated by you." The product page says "Once you set it up, it runs on the schedule you choose," like every Monday or when a new lead comes in. Both say you approve before anything sends or pays. The product page puts it this way: "Anything that sends or pays still waits for your approval." On data, Anthropic says "We don't train on your data by default on our Team and Enterprise Plans." That line names Team and Enterprise only.

What it costs

Anthropic's product page doesn't list a separate fee. It says to use "Pro for one person, Team for a business with more than one." Prices on claude.com/pricing, checked October 23, 2026: Pro is $20 a month billed monthly ($17 a month billed yearly). Team standard seats are $25 per seat billed monthly ($20 billed yearly). Max starts at $100 a month. You still pay for the tools you connect, like QuickBooks. Before you count on a connector, check that it shows up in your Claude plan.

FAQ

What is Claude for Small Business?

A set of connectors and ready-to-run workflows that Anthropic launched on May 13, 2026. It puts Claude to work inside tools you already use. The launch post named Intuit QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Docusign, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, plus 15 ready-to-run workflows. Examples it named: planning payroll, closing the month, invoice chasing, lead triage, contract review and running a campaign. The product page now also lists Shopify, Stripe and Gusto.

How much does Claude for Small Business cost?

Anthropic's product page doesn't list a separate fee. It says to use "Pro for one person, Team for a business with more than one." As of October 23, 2026, claude.com/pricing lists Pro at $20 a month billed monthly and Team standard seats at $25 per seat billed monthly, and less if billed yearly. You still pay for the tools you connect, like QuickBooks or PayPal.

Does Claude for Small Business run tasks automatically, without approval?

It can run on a schedule, but it doesn't send or pay without you. Anthropic's product page says "Once you set it up, it runs on the schedule you choose" and "Anything that sends or pays still waits for your approval." Anthropic also says "We don't train on your data by default on our Team and Enterprise Plans."

Is this the same as making my business visible to AI search engines?

No. Claude for Small Business is a tool you use to run your operations. It has nothing to do with whether ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity can find and read your business when customers ask.
