What we actually check

Not a generic checklist. Google names exactly three ranking factors for local results in its own documentation — no more, no fewer, whatever a "200 factors" listicle tells you:

FactorWhat it means
RelevanceDoes your profile match what the searcher typed, starting with your primary category
DistanceHow far you are from the searcher — not something you can move
ProminenceHow well-known you are: review volume, review recency, and links

Category correctness is the one owners get wrong most: local-ranking studies rate the right primary category as the single strongest positive factor, and the wrong one as the strongest negative.
