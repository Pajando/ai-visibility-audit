What we actually check

Not a generic checklist. Google says local results are "mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity", and it explains the third one under the heading "Prominence." Those three are where we start:

FactorWhat it means
RelevanceHow well your profile matches what someone searched. Google says complete, detailed info helps.
DistanceHow far you are from the person searching. Not something you can move.
ProminenceHow well-known you are. Google names how many websites link to you and how many reviews you have.

My take: the primary category is the one owners get wrong most, and it's the first thing we check.
