What the free scan doesn't check

Our free AO Scan is real, and it's free because it only measures one thing: can a machine read your site. It checks whether your site loads, whether it has structured data, a sitemap, an AI-instructions file, visible prices, and real text content. It does not check whether your links actually work, whether your site looks right on a phone, whether it's held together with technology nobody's supported in a decade, or whether the writing on it says anything a customer would trust. That's a different question, and it needs a person looking, not just a script.

If the answer is "replace it"

Sometimes patching an old site costs more than starting over — a site built on technology nobody maintains, or with no mobile layout at all, is often more expensive to fix piece by piece than to rebuild once. The audit tells you honestly which situation you're actually in, before you spend anything on a rebuild you didn't need, or patch a site that needed replacing.
