A speed tool, not a substance tool

No time to write posts and emails? What AI marketing tools are actually good for

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 8, 2026

If you're writing posts and emails at 10 PM after closing, AI tools can give you a first draft fast. That's what this page is about. It's a different question from whether customers can find you when they ask AI. Writing your newsletter with AI doesn't change how easily ChatGPT can read your website, and you don't need an AI writing tool to be readable.

My take: most "best AI marketing tools" lists rank the same big names with more confidence than evidence. Here's a more useful version: what these tools do well, and the catch most lists skip.

The tradeoff most roundups skip

A 2024 study by researchers at Princeton and IIT Delhi, published at the KDD 2024 conference, tested ways to change a web page so AI answer engines show more of it (read the paper, more on our GEO page). Adding cited sources, quotations and statistics did best: up to 40% more visibility on the authors' own measure. Keyword stuffing, the authors wrote, offered "little to no improvement." Most tests ran on the authors' own test engine, with a smaller test on Perplexity. It did not test emails or ads. My take: the lesson still carries. Specific beats generic, whether the reader is a customer or a machine.

So the biggest win usually isn't a different tool. It's what you give the tool before you hit generate: real prices, a real story, what's actually true about this week's special. If a competitor could paste your post onto their own page unchanged, it's too generic. That's my take, not a study.

FAQ

What can AI marketing tools actually do for a small business?

Write first drafts faster: social captions, email campaigns, ad copy options, blog outlines, product descriptions. My take: general tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini handle most of this, so you don't need ten subscriptions to start.

Are AI marketing tools worth it for a small business?

For speed, often yes. For quality, it depends on what you feed it. A 2024 study by Princeton and IIT Delhi researchers found that keyword stuffing offered "little to no improvement" in how much AI answer engines showed a page, while adding sources, quotes and statistics helped most. That study tested web pages, not emails or ads. My take: speed without real details just makes generic content faster.

What's the difference between AI marketing tools and AI search visibility?

AI marketing tools help you create content: posts, emails, ad copy. AI search visibility is whether tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity can find and read your business when a customer asks. They're separate. Writing faster with AI doesn't make your website easier for AI to read, and being readable doesn't require any AI writing tool. This page is mostly about the first question.

What AI email marketing tools should a small business use?

Start with the platform you already use. Mailchimp and Constant Contact both have built-in AI writing; check which plan includes it. My take: an AI draft with your real offer, real details and your own voice beats a generic AI draft, no matter which tool wrote it.
