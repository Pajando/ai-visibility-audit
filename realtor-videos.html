Honest limits

What we can and can't guarantee: we guarantee the videos and photos are made, delivered on time, and match what you approved. We don't guarantee views, leads, or how any platform treats what you post. Nobody selling this honestly can promise that, and we won't pretend otherwise.

California's AB 723 (bill text): since January 1, 2026, if a broker or agent uses a listing image changed with editing software or AI to add, remove or change things (furniture, flooring, paint, landscaping, views and more), the ad needs a clear statement on or next to the image saying it was altered. That statement has to point to a public link, URL or QR code where the original, unaltered image can be seen. Simple fixes like lighting, color correction, cropping or straightening don't count. The law is written for the broker or agent advertising the property, and it may also reach images made for them by people like us. Either way, we'll flag every image in your package that needs the tag. My take: ask your broker how this applies to video too.

Do AI-generated listing photos need a disclosure in California?

Often, yes. Under California's AB 723 (in effect since January 1, 2026), if a broker or agent uses an image changed with software or AI to add, remove or change parts of the property, such as virtual staging, the ad needs a clear statement on or next to the image saying it was altered, plus a public link, URL or QR code to the original image. Lighting, color correction and cropping alone don't count. We'll tell you plainly which images in your package need that tag.
