Do AI-generated listing photos need a disclosure in California?

If a photo is digitally altered in a way that could mislead a buyer about a property's condition or features - virtual staging, for example - California's AB 723 (effective January 1, 2026) requires a clear disclosure on or next to the image, and the original unaltered photo has to be kept and produced on request.
