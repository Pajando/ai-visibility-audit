What OpenAI actually says

Customers are asking ChatGPT where to go. Will it find your business?

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 2, 2026

The short answer: nobody can make ChatGPT recommend you, and OpenAI says so: "Placement is not guaranteed." What OpenAI does tell site owners is short. To be eligible, "allow OAI-Searchbot to crawl the site," and make sure your host or CDN lets OpenAI's search bot through. ChatGPT search "sometimes partners with other search providers," and it may use a person's rough location from their IP address to find local results. Everything else on this page is either one outside study or labeled as my take. (Source: OpenAI Help, "Searching the web with ChatGPT", read October 23, 2026.)

Search "how to get ChatGPT to recommend my business" and you'll find plenty of confident advice. Very little of it links to what OpenAI itself says. Here's what OpenAI says, one dated study, and what I'd check first as a local owner.

What about schema, reviews and listings?

OpenAI's help page doesn't say ChatGPT favors schema markup, reviews or matching listings. My take: they still help any machine read and check your facts, so treat them as good housekeeping, not a known ranking factor. OpenAI also warns: "Search results and citations can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect." So once in a while, ask ChatGPT about your own business and see what it says. If it gets something wrong, fix that fact everywhere it shows up online.

Nobody can guarantee ChatGPT, or any AI tool, will recommend a specific business on a specific day. OpenAI's own words: "Placement is not guaranteed." What you can control is whether its search bot is allowed to read your site, and whether your facts are easy to find. Anyone selling a guarantee is selling something they don't control.

FAQ

How do you get ChatGPT to recommend your business?

Nobody can make it happen. OpenAI says "Placement is not guaranteed," and that ChatGPT "ranks search results using multiple factors intended to help users find relevant, reliable information." The one thing it tells site owners to do: allow OAI-SearchBot to crawl your site, and make sure your host or CDN lets OpenAI's search bot through. ChatGPT search also sometimes uses other search providers. One 2025 study (Seer Interactive, about 100 questions) found more than 87% of its citations matched Bing's top 20 results. My take: get those basics right, keep your facts clear and the same everywhere, then check what ChatGPT says about you.

What does ChatGPT actually check before it recommends a business?

OpenAI doesn't publish a list. Its help page says ChatGPT "ranks search results using multiple factors," may use a person's rough location from their IP address for local results, and "sometimes partners with other search providers," rewriting the question into targeted searches. OpenAI's own example: "good restaurants near me" in the San Francisco area may become "top restaurants San Francisco." OpenAI does not say it favors structured data. My take: schema and matching listings still help any machine read your facts.

ChatGPT already recommends my business. How did that happen?

There's no way to know for sure. OpenAI says it uses "multiple factors" and doesn't list them. My take: before you redesign your site or change your listings, write down what's in place now (your robots.txt, your schema, your Google and Yelp details) so you can tell if a change breaks something. Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?
