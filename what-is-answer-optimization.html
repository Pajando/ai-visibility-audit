What is Answer Optimization (AO)? AO vs SEO, AEO and GEO, in plain English

By Alejandro Ojeda · Be the Answer · aoaudit.com

Answer Optimization (AO) is the practice of making your business the answer AI engines give. When a customer asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, or Google AI Mode “who should I use for ___ near me,” AO is everything you do so the AI can find, verify, and recommend you. The industry also calls this AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). Three names, one practice.

Search is changing shape. For twenty years the goal was a ranked list of blue links, and the discipline that won those rankings was SEO. Now a billion people use Google's AI Mode, ChatGPT recommends specific businesses by name, and AI agents can call and book on a customer's behalf. The customer doesn't scroll a list anymore. They get an answer. Usually one or two names. There is no page two of an answer.

That shift needs its own discipline. We call it Answer Optimization because that's what it literally is: optimizing to be the answer.

Two honest observations about that table:

1. AEO, GEO, and AO are substantially the same practice. AEO and GEO are the industry's technical terms; AO is the plain-English name we use. We didn't invent a new discipline. We gave the existing one a name your customers can actually say. If you search for AEO advice or GEO research, it applies here, and vice versa.

2. AO doesn't replace SEO. It sits on top of it. AI engines still crawl the web the way search engines do. A site that's invisible to Google's crawler is invisible to Gemini too. The SEO fundamentals are the floor: crawlable pages, clear structure, fast loading. AO adds what AI engines specifically need above that floor.

What AI engines actually need from your business

This is the practical core of AO. From scanning real business websites every day at aoaudit.com, the pattern is consistent. AI engines reward businesses that are:

1. Readable by machines

Schema markup (structured data that states your name, services, prices, hours in a format AI parses directly), an llms.txt file, and crawler access for AI bots in your robots.txt. Most local businesses have none of these. In our scans, the single most common failure is a site that looks fine to humans and reads as nearly blank to a bot.

2. Verifiable across sources

AI engines cross-check. Your website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business (formerly Business Connect), and Yelp should agree on your name, address, phone, and hours. Disagreement reads as unreliability, and unreliable businesses don't get recommended.

3. Answer-shaped

Customers ask AI full questions: “how much does a fade cost in Napa,” “do they take walk-ins.” Businesses that publish real answers give the AI something to quote. That means prices on the site, an FAQ that matches real questions, and booking links a bot can follow. Businesses that hide prices behind “call us” give it nothing.

Common questions

What is Answer Optimization (AO)?

Answer Optimization (AO) is the practice of making your business the answer AI engines give. When a customer asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google AI Mode a question like 'best barber near me,' AO is everything you do so the AI can find, verify and recommend your business. That means machine-readable structure (schema markup, llms.txt, crawler access), complete business profiles, published pricing, and content that answers real customer questions.

Is Answer Optimization the same as SEO?

They overlap but aim at different judges. SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links on a search results page. AO optimizes for a single spoken or written answer from an AI. Good SEO fundamentals still matter, including crawlable pages, fast loading and clear structure. AO adds what AI engines specifically need: structured data they can quote, facts they can verify across sources, and answers phrased the way customers actually ask.

What is the difference between AEO, GEO, and AO?

They are three names for substantially the same practice. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) are the industry's technical terms. AO (Answer Optimization) is the plain-English name we use at aoaudit.com. Whatever you call it, the work is identical: make your business readable, verifiable and recommendable to AI engines.
