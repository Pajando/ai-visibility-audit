The one thing Perplexity does differently

A customer asks Perplexity who to call. Can it even use your site?

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 6, 2026

A customer asks Perplexity for a plumber or a taqueria. If your site isn't one of the sources it links, that customer may never see it. Perplexity's help center says "Each answer includes numbered citations linking to the original sources" (Perplexity). The part you control: let its crawler in, and put your real facts (services, hours, prices, town) in plain text. Perplexity doesn't publish how it picks sources, so nobody can promise you a spot.

My take: a lot of "get cited by Perplexity" advice is regular SEO advice with a new name, and some of it claims to know a ranking formula Perplexity has never published. Here's what Perplexity itself says, and where it stops.

The upside: you can check it yourself. Ask Perplexity the question your customers ask, like "best brake shop in Vacaville," and read the numbered sources. Is your site there? Is a competitor's? My take: a page with real specifics gives it more to quote than a page of slogans.

What Perplexity hasn't published, said honestly

Perplexity's help center says it uses AI to "search the internet in real-time." It also runs a crawler, PerplexityBot, so it doesn't only fetch pages live. What it does not publish is how it picks or orders sources. I could not find a Perplexity page that lists ranking factors, so anyone selling you "the Perplexity algorithm" is guessing. Two crawler facts from its docs: Perplexity recommends allowing PerplexityBot in your robots.txt, and its user-triggered fetcher, Perplexity-User, "generally ignores robots.txt rules" because a person asked for the page (Perplexity docs).

One more split. Using Perplexity to research your own business is one question. Showing up in your customers' answers is another. This page is about the second one: being found.

FAQ

How do small businesses get recommended by Perplexity?

Perplexity says it searches the internet in real time and that "Each answer includes numbered citations linking to the original sources." It doesn't publish how it picks them. What you control: allow PerplexityBot in your robots.txt (Perplexity recommends it), and put your services, hours, prices and town in plain text. No one can promise you'll be cited.

Is Perplexity different from ChatGPT or Google for local businesses?

Perplexity shows numbered citations in each answer, so you can see which pages it used. OpenAI says ChatGPT search ranks results using "multiple factors" and "Placement is not guaranteed." Google shows ranked links and sometimes an AI Overview. My take: on Perplexity it's easier to check whether you're a source, because the links are right there.

Does Perplexity publish exactly how it ranks sources?

No. I could not find a Perplexity page that lists its ranking factors or their weights. Its help center says it searches the web in real time and cites sources, and its crawler docs explain PerplexityBot. Anyone who claims to know the exact formula is guessing. My take: clear, current facts in plain text are the safe bet on every platform.

Can I use Perplexity for my small business, separate from getting cited by it?

Yes. That's a different question. Using Perplexity as a research tool for your business is one thing. Showing up when customers ask it is another. This page is about the second one: being found, not doing the finding.
