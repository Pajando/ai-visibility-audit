The one thing Perplexity does differently

How do small businesses get recommended by Perplexity?

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 6, 2026

Perplexity always shows its work: every answer carries numbered citations linking straight to the pages it pulled from. That single design choice changes what actually earns a mention — a page has to give Perplexity something concrete and quotable, not just rank well. Clear facts in readable text, backed by structured data, is what gets picked up. Vague marketing copy gives it nothing to cite, no matter how polished it looks.

Most of what's written about "getting cited by Perplexity" either repeats generic SEO advice with the word "Perplexity" swapped in, or claims to know the exact ranking algorithm, which nobody outside the company actually does. Here's the honest version of both halves.

That visibility cuts both ways. It's easier to see whether you're being cited — search your own business question on Perplexity and check the source list. It also means there's no ambiguity about whether the content behind the citation was worth citing: thin, generic pages don't hold up next to sources with real specifics.

What Perplexity hasn't published, said honestly

Perplexity has talked publicly about weighing relevance, freshness, and credibility in what it surfaces, and its retrieval happens in real time against the live web rather than a fixed index it built once. What it hasn't published is the exact formula — how those factors are weighted, or what tips a borderline page into the cited set. Anyone selling you the precise algorithm is guessing, same as with any other AI engine. What holds up across every account of how it works, official or not: clear, factual, structured, current content is the direction that helps, everywhere, not a Perplexity-specific trick.

One more distinction worth making: using Perplexity to research your own business is a different question from getting your business cited in other people's answers. FAQ

How do small businesses get recommended by Perplexity?

Perplexity answers by searching the live web and citing sources — every answer shows numbered citations you can click through to the original page. That means being crawlable and having clear, extractable content matters more than any trick. A page with plain facts (hours, services, location) in readable text and structured data gives Perplexity something concrete to cite; a page that's all marketing language and no specifics gives it nothing to quote.

Is Perplexity different from ChatGPT or Google for local businesses?

The visible difference is that Perplexity always shows its sources as numbered citations, right in the answer — you can see exactly which pages it pulled from. ChatGPT's citation behavior varies by mode, and Google's classic results are ranked links rather than a single synthesized answer. Perplexity's whole product is built around citing, which makes source quality and clarity matter more directly than on either of the others.

Does Perplexity publish exactly how it ranks sources?

Not in the detail businesses would want. Perplexity has talked publicly about weighing relevance, freshness, and credibility, and its retrieval is real-time rather than a fixed index, but the specific ranking formula isn't published. Anyone who tells you the exact algorithm is guessing or selling something — the honest version is that clear, factual, structured, up-to-date content is the direction that consistently helps, everywhere.

Can I use Perplexity for my small business, separate from getting cited by it?

Yes, that's a different use case — using Perplexity as a research tool for your own business, rather than trying to be cited in other people's answers. Both are real, but they're separate questions with separate answers, and this page is about the second one: being found, not doing the finding.
