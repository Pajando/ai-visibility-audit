If Google can't tell what your video says

Made a great video and nobody finds it? Here's what video SEO actually is.

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 9, 2026

Video SEO means helping Google and AI tools tell what your video is about. That means a clear title and description, real captions or a transcript, chapter timestamps, and VideoObject structured data (name, thumbnail, upload date, length). Some AI can now process video itself: Google's Gemini docs say "Gemini models can process videos," sound and picture. But for Search, Google says "we recommend providing metadata about the video" to make it easier to find. My take: if your video has no words around it, you're making every machine guess.

My take: captions are a big reason YouTube keeps coming up in talk about AI. YouTube can add automatic captions, which turn speech into text. But YouTube's own help page says automatic captions "may not be ready" right after upload and "might misrepresent the spoken content." So check yours. Don't assume.

What actually makes a video machine-readable

ElementWhy it matters to Google and AI tools
Transcript / captionsTurns what you say into text that search engines and AI tools can read. Check auto-captions for mistakes, especially names, prices and your town.
VideoObject schemaStates the video's name, description, thumbnail, upload date, and duration in a format a machine parses directly instead of guessing
Chapter timestampsGoogle Search can show "key moments" from a video. Google says it will "prioritize key moments set by you," through structured data or timestamps in your YouTube description.

The trend claim, and its actual limit

You may have seen the claim that YouTube is cited in 16% of AI answers and Reddit in 10%. It comes from an Adweek report: "YouTube appeared as a cited source in 16% of LLM answers over the past six months, compared with 10% for Reddit." Those numbers come from one company, Bluefish. The article gives no sample size and doesn't name the AI tools measured. Adweek links the rise to "transcripts, explainers, and other information associated with videos on YouTube." My take: the direction makes sense. The exact 16% is one company's number, not an industry fact.

FAQ

What is video SEO?

Video SEO means helping search engines and AI tools tell what's in a video: a clear title and description, real captions or a transcript, chapter timestamps, and VideoObject structured data (schema.org markup with the video's name, thumbnail, upload date and length). None of it changes the video. It changes how easily a machine can tell what the video is about.

Why does YouTube keep showing up in AI answers?

Nobody outside these companies knows the full reason. My take: many YouTube videos have captions, and text is easy for AI tools to read and quote. Some AI can also process video directly: Google's Gemini docs say you can pass YouTube links to Gemini. We haven't found a Google page saying AI Overviews favor YouTube, so we won't claim that.

Is it true that YouTube is now cited more than Reddit in AI answers?

That claim (16% of AI answers citing YouTube versus 10% for Reddit) comes from one company, Bluefish, as reported by Adweek. The article gives no sample size and doesn't name which AI tools were measured, beyond "the past six months." My take: the direction makes sense. Treat the exact number as one company's estimate, not an industry fact.
