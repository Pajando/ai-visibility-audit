The real feature, not a rumor

No time to answer reviews or fix your Google listing? Here's what Google's Gemini can do now

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · August 2, 2026

The short answer: on June 10, 2026, Google announced that Gemini can connect to your Business Profile. Google said it would begin rolling out globally, except the EEA and UK. Once connected, it can draft review replies, update your hours and details, and show how your profile is doing. Google's help page says you must manage only one verified profile, sign in at gemini.google.com with a personal Google account, and have Keep Activity turned on. If you just want help writing your business description, you don't need a special tool. Any AI chatbot can draft one if you give it your real details.

My take: search results on this topic mix real news with "AI tools for your business" lists full of unrelated products. Below is what Google's own announcement and help page say, plus the part that's simpler than most articles make it sound.

Want AI to just write your description? Skip the "generator" search

My take: a lot of "AI Google Business Profile description generator" tools are a form wrapped around something you can do yourself. Open any AI chatbot (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) and ask it to write your description. Give it your real details: what you do, your city, your specialty, how long you've been open, and what actually makes you different. No special tool needed.

My take: if you give AI a generic prompt, you get a generic description back, the kind that could fit any competitor in your category. It's fine as a first draft. The fix isn't a better generator. It's better input: give it your real facts, then edit before you publish.

My take: Google's own tool has one edge here. It reads your actual profile data, so it starts from your real info. A general chatbot only knows what you tell it. Either way, AI can still get things wrong, so check every line against the facts before you publish.

FAQ

Is there really an AI tool for Google Business Profile?

Yes. On June 10, 2026, Google announced a Gemini connection for Business Profile. Google said it would begin rolling out globally, except the EEA and UK. Google's help page says you must manage only one verified profile, use a personal Google account at gemini.google.com, be 18 or over, and have Keep Activity on. Once connected, it can show how your profile is doing, draft review replies, and update your hours and details. It comes from Google, not a third party.

What does the Google Business Profile AI agent actually do?

Based on Google's announcement and help page, it shows performance (search impressions, direction requests, calls, engagement and the search words people used), drafts review replies and sums up what reviews say, updates your hours, contact details, menus, photos and other details, and creates Update, Offer or Event posts. Google didn't list a price.

You don't need a special tool.
