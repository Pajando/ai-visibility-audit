Can AI tools even see your business? What AI visibility is, and how to check yours free

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · July 24, 2026

AI visibility is whether AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and the agents built on them) can find, read, and check your business when a customer asks. It's not a feeling; it's measurable: crawler access, structured data, machine-readable content, published prices, and profiles that agree. You can check yours free in about 60 seconds.

Google says AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly active users (May 2026). My take: when an AI answers "who's the best near me" with one or two names, you want it to have clear facts about you, because there is no page two of an answer. And here's the part most owners never hear: having a website doesn't mean AI can see it. In our scans of 257 local businesses (July 26 to August 15, 2026), 52 had no website at all, and 37 of the 205 websites we found could not be opened by our automated reader.

What AI visibility actually consists of

My take: think of it as four questions a machine has to answer about you. None of them is about your design.

The machine's questionWhat it checks
Can I read you?robots.txt and llms.txt permissions, whether your content exists as text (not just JavaScript or images), indexability
Can I verify you?Schema markup (your identity in machine language), business profiles on Google, Bing, Apple and Yelp that agree on name, phone, and hours
Can I quote you?Published prices, a real FAQ, answer-shaped content. AI can only repeat facts you've made public
Can I act on you?Booking links and machine-readable contact info: what an AI agent would need to call or book for a customer

What real scans actually find

This isn't theoretical. 112 of the 168 readable sites we scanned in Napa and Solano counties had no llms.txt file (67%) (Our own scans, July 26 to August 15, 2026). The full list, engine by engine, lives in our AI SEO scan-log report. The short version: before you blame a competitor, check whether machines can read your own site. That part you can fix.

The manual spot-checks (also free, also revealing)

Then ask the money question: "best [what you do] in [your area]", and see whose names come back. Finally, visit yoursite.com/robots.txt: if you've never looked at that file, you don't know what it tells AI crawlers.

Honest limit: no tool (ours included) can guarantee what an AI says on a given day. A visibility score measures whether machines can read your site. It can't promise a mention. FAQ

What is AI visibility?

AI visibility is whether AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and the agents built on them) can find, read, and check your business when a customer asks. It is measurable: crawler access, structured data, machine-readable content, published prices, and business profiles that agree with each other all affect how easily AI can read you. Is AI visibility the same as SEO?

No. SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links on a search results page; AI visibility is whether an AI tool can find, read, and check your business when it builds a single answer. Good SEO fundamentals help. My take: AI visibility also leans on things classic SEO often skipped: AI-crawler access, facts machines can quote, business profiles that agree with each other, and answer-shaped content.

What is a good AO Score?

The AO Score runs 0–100 on verified checks only: 85 and above is agent-ready; 65–84 is visible with gaps; 40–64 is half-readable; below 40 is hard for machines to read. A low score doesn't mean the business is bad. It usually means nobody ever made the site easy for machines to read.

How often should I check my AI visibility?

Re-scan after every meaningful site change, and re-test AI answers monthly: ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity your customers' real questions and see whether you are mentioned. My take: AI answers shift over time, so visibility is a habit, not a one-time fix.
