Why this matters more here than almost anywhere

Napa Valley runs on recommendations. Millions of visitors a year — plus every local deciding where to eat, who to hire, and where to take their car — used to search, scroll, and pick. Now they ask: "best tasting room for a first visit," "who can fix a fence in American Canyon," "dog groomer near Yountville." The AI answers with one or two names, spoken like a concierge. There is no page two of an answer — and in a valley where every block has three excellent options, the business the machines can verify is the one that gets said out loud.

Here's what we find when we scan real local businesses: most are invisible to the machines their customers now ask. Not because they're bad businesses — because nobody ever made their website and profiles machine-readable. That's fixable, usually in weeks.
