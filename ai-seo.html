The short version: AI SEO is making your website and profiles easy for AI tools to find, read, and check, so they have what they need to mention your business. It isn't a bag of tricks. Each engine answers from what it can read, so the work is making your business readable: crawler access, schema markup, published prices, consistent profiles. We call the full practice Answer Optimization (AO).

The failures we see most

These come from scanning real local-business websites, not from theory:

1. No AI instructions at all

112 of the 168 readable sites we scanned in Napa and Solano counties had no llms.txt file (67%). (Our own scans, July 26 to August 15, 2026). Two out of three had no llms.txt when we checked. My take: it takes minutes to add. I can't point you to an AI company that promises to read it, so treat it as cheap insurance, not a sure thing.

2. The site looks fine to humans and reads blank to bots

My take: this is the costliest one. Some sites build every word with JavaScript, so a bot that doesn't run the script sees an almost empty page. The owner sees a nice site in the browser and has no idea. In our scans (July 26 to August 15, 2026), 37 of the 205 business websites could not be opened by our automated reader at all, for one reason or another. Fix this first, because no tool can quote what it can't read.

3. Schema markup missing or broken

Structured data is how you state your name, services, prices, and hours in a format machines read directly. Some sites have it and it's broken, including, at one point, ours. Google says there is no special schema you need for its AI Overviews or AI Mode. My take: valid schema is still cheap and helps any machine read your facts. It just isn't a magic switch.

4. Prices and answers hidden behind “call us”

My take: customers ask AI full questions: “how much does a fade cost,” “do they take walk-ins.” An engine can only quote what's published. Sites that publish real prices and a real FAQ give the AI something to say about them; sites that hide everything give it nothing.

5. Profiles that disagree with each other

My take: different hours or an old phone number across your website, Google, Bing, Apple, and Yelp make you look unreliable to anyone, person or machine, who checks more than one source.

The order I'd fix them in

From the gaps above, in the order I'd do them: (1) unblock and invite the crawlers (robots.txt + llms.txt), (2) install valid schema markup, (3) claim Bing Places and complete your Google Business Profile, (4) publish prices and a genuine FAQ, (5) make every profile agree. That's the backbone of our audit reports. Your own gaps and order come from your own scan.

What nobody can honestly promise: you can't buy a mention, and no one controls what an AI says on a given day. Answers vary by user, session, and engine. Anyone guaranteeing “#1 in ChatGPT” is selling weather. What you can control is whether your site is easy to read and your facts match everywhere. Readability is the part we measure, and our score counts only what we can verify.

FAQ

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is making your website and business profiles easy for AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Mode) to find, read, and check, so they have what they need to mention your business in their answers. It builds on classic SEO (crawlable pages, clear structure) and adds things that help AI tools read you: structured data (schema markup, helpful but not required by Google), AI-crawler access, an llms.txt file, published prices, and consistent business profiles. The practice is also called Answer Optimization (AO), AEO, or GEO.

How do I show up in ChatGPT recommendations?

Start with Bing. In a small 2025 study, Seer Interactive found more than 87% of ChatGPT search citations matched Bing's top 20 results for the same question (about 100 questions, which the authors call a limited sample). So the practical moves are: make sure Bing can crawl your site, claim your Bing Places listing at bing.com/forbusiness, allow OpenAI's crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot) in robots.txt, and publish facts (schema, prices, FAQ) a machine can quote. Nobody can promise ChatGPT will name you.

Does Bing really matter for AI visibility?

My take: yes. In Seer Interactive's 2025 study, more than 87% of ChatGPT search citations matched Bing's top 20 results, so a business Bing can't see starts behind in ChatGPT's web answers. A free Bing Places listing is one of the cheapest fixes there is.

How is AI SEO different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO competes for a ranked list of links; AI SEO competes to be the answer itself. The basics overlap: if a search crawler can't read your site, an AI crawler probably can't either. AI SEO adds layers like these: schema markup AI can parse, explicit AI-crawler permissions, llms.txt, and facts (prices, hours, services) stated in text a bot can verify rather than hidden behind 'call us.'
