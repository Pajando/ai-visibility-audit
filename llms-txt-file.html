llms.txt lives at yoursite.com/llms.txt. It's a short text file with your site's name, a quick summary, and links to your key pages. The pattern, if you're writing your own

We wrote the file above by hand. It follows the shape in the llms.txt proposal by Jeremy Howard (September 3, 2024): a heading with the site's name (the only required part), a short summary in a quote block, then sections of links, each with a one-line note.

It's a proposal, not an official standard, and nobody checks that you follow it. That's why real llms.txt files online rarely look the same.

How to check if you actually have one

Type yoursite.com/llms.txt into your browser. If you get a "page not found" (404) error, you don't have one. That's the whole check. Or run a free AO Audit scan. It looks for llms.txt as one of 21 automated checks, including schema markup and AI-crawler access.

How to make your own

Open a plain text editor. Start with a heading (# Your Business Name). Add a short quote line (> ...) on what you do and where. Then link your most important pages: homepage, pricing, anything with real facts about the business. Save it as llms.txt and put it in your site's root folder, next to robots.txt. No generator, no plugin. My take: about 20 minutes for most small businesses.

Honest limit: llms.txt is young, and there's no promise any AI tool reads it. Google says plainly that for its AI Overviews and AI Mode, "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup to appear in these features" (Google Search Central). So don't expect it to help you on Google. My take: it takes about 20 minutes, costs nothing, and some other AI tools may use it. Treat it as a small extra, not a fix.

FAQ

What is llms.txt?

A short text file at yoursite.com/llms.txt, written in plain Markdown, that sums up a site for AI tools. It comes from a 2024 proposal at llmstxt.org, not an official standard. Robots.txt tells crawlers where they may go. llms.txt is a map, not a gate. Google says you don't need AI text files to appear in its AI features.

What does a real llms.txt file example look like?

Short and plain. The llmstxt.org proposal requires one thing: a heading with the site's name. Most files then add a one-line summary in a quote block and a few sections of links, each with a one-line note. aoaudit.com's file follows that shape.

How do I check if my website has an llms.txt file?

Type yoursite.com/llms.txt into a browser. A 404 (page not found) means there's no file. A free AO Audit scan also checks for it automatically, as one of 21 checks that include schema markup and AI-crawler access.

How do I create an llms.txt file?

Open any text editor. Write a heading with your business name, a short summary of what you do, and links to your key pages with one-line notes. Save it as llms.txt and upload it to your site's root folder. No generator tool needed. My take: most small businesses can write one in about 20 minutes.
