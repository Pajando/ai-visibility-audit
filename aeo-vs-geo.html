AEO vs GEO vs SEO: what's the difference — and what actually matters

By Alejandro Ojeda · The AO Audit · July 24, 2026

The short answer: SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links — its judge is Google's algorithm. AEO and GEO are two names for nearly the same newer practice: getting cited inside AI answers — their judges are ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. And behind all three sits one plain-English goal: be the answer AI gives your customer. That's Answer Optimization (AO).

If you run a business and keep hearing these acronyms, here's the honest decoder — including the part most articles skip: two of the three terms mean almost the same thing, and none of them is the goal. They're tactics. The goal has a simpler name.

AEO vs GEO: the difference nobody can show you

Ask ten marketers the difference between AEO and GEO and you'll get ten confident answers that don't survive contact with the checklist — because in practice, the work is identical: open your site to AI crawlers, install schema, publish answer-shaped content, keep your profiles consistent, earn real reviews and mentions. AEO is what the SEO industry called the shift; GEO is what the researchers called it. Same building, two signs on the door.

SEO vs the rest: the difference that's real

This one matters. SEO's contest is a list — ten blue links, and page two exists (nobody visits, but it exists). The AI contest is an answer — one or two names, spoken or written, and there is no page two of an answer. A billion people now search with Google's AI Mode; ChatGPT recommends businesses by name and sends shoppers to their sites. The skills overlap, but the judges changed — and the judges now read different signals: ChatGPT's search citations trace overwhelmingly to Bing's index (~87%), Gemini reads your Google Business Profile as a data source, and Perplexity quotes pages that answer plainly.

So which one do you "do"?

None of them, by name. You do the checklist they all share — by leverage order: open the doors (robots.txt + llms.txt) → valid schema → claim Google, Bing, Apple, Yelp → publish real prices and a real FAQ → make every source agree. Then you measure the thing that actually matters: your AI visibility — whether machines can find, verify, and recommend you. That's checkable, free, in about 60 seconds.

Honest limit: whatever the acronym, nobody can guarantee what an AI answers on a given day. What you control is whether every engine can read, verify, and quote you. Anyone selling guaranteed #1 in ChatGPT — under any acronym — is selling weather.

FAQ

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

Almost none in practice — two names for the same work of getting cited by AI engines. AEO came from the SEO industry; GEO from academic research. The tactics overlap nearly completely.

Is AEO the same as SEO?

No. SEO targets a ranked list of links; AEO targets the single answer an AI gives. SEO is the foundation; AEO adds machine-readability requirements SEO never tested.

Do I need SEO, AEO, or GEO?

You need the goal behind all three: being the answer AI gives your customer — AO. The work is one checklist, whatever you call it.

Which matters more in 2026?

SEO is the foundation (it feeds the indexes AI reads), AEO/GEO is the building, being the answer is the point. The weight is shifting toward the answer.
