The three things Google actually weighs

Ignore the "200 ranking factors" folklore. For local results, Google's own help page says results are mainly based on three things: relevance (does your profile match what was searched), distance (how far you are from the searcher), and prominence (how well-known you are, including links and how many reviews you have). You can't move your building. My take: that leaves relevance and prominence: an exact primary category, a complete profile, fresh reviews, and a website that corroborates all of it in machine-readable form.

The part nobody mentions: Google is only half the problem now

Everything above decides whether Google shows you. But your customers changed how they ask. Google says AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly active users (Google, May 2026). Google says a page has to be in its search index to show up as a link in its AI answers (Google's guide). For ChatGPT search, more than 87% of citations matched Bing's top 20 results in one study (Seer Interactive, Feb 2025). My take: AI answers are short and name only a few businesses. There is no page two of an answer. So if you've vanished from Google Maps, check how AI tools see you too. Lots of local sites skip even the small stuff: 112 of the 168 readable sites we scanned in Napa and Solano County had no llms.txt file (67%) (our own scans, July 26 to August 15, 2026). That file is optional, and Google says its AI features need no special optimizations, so treat it as a small sign, not the fix.

So the real question isn't just "how do I get back on Google." It's "can Google and AI tools read and check what my business says, anywhere?"

FAQ

Why is my business not showing up on Google Maps?

Common causes: your Google Business Profile is unverified, suspended, or was never created; your business information (name, address, phone) doesn't match across the web; or other businesses outrank you in your area. Google's own help page says local results are mainly based on three factors: relevance, distance, and prominence. Start by checking your profile status at google.com/business, then verify your information matches everywhere.

Why did my business stop showing up on Google after verification?

Google says verification reviews can take up to 5 business days. If the profile still doesn't show, check for mismatched details, a wrong category, or a policy problem. Confirm the profile shows 'verified' status, that your primary category is exactly right, and that your website and profiles agree on name, address, and phone. If the profile was suspended, Google provides a reinstatement request process.

Why is my business not showing up on Google anymore when it used to?

Something changed: a competitor strengthened their profile, your reviews went quiet, your website became unreadable to crawlers after a redesign, or a suspension or edit affected the listing. Compare your profile against the top-ranked competitor's and check how readable your website is. Both are free to check.

If my business doesn't show on Google, is it invisible to ChatGPT too?

If the cause is your website, likely yes for Google's AI: Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode only show pages that are indexed and eligible for a snippet in Search. For ChatGPT search, one study found more than 87% of its citations matched Bing's top 20 results (Seer Interactive, Feb 2025), so check Bing too.
