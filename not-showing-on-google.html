The three things Google actually weighs

Ignore the "200 ranking factors" folklore — for local results, Google's own documentation names exactly three: relevance (does your profile match what was searched), distance (how far you are from the searcher), and prominence (how well-known you are — reviews and links). You can't move your building, so the whole game is relevance and prominence: an exact primary category, a complete profile, fresh reviews, and a website that corroborates all of it in machine-readable form.

The part nobody mentions: Google is only half the problem now

Everything above decides whether Google shows you. But your customers changed how they ask: a billion people now use Google's AI Mode, and ChatGPT recommends businesses by name. Those systems read the same signals — your profile, your consistency, your site's machine-readability — and answer with one or two names. There is no page two of an answer. So if you've vanished from Google Maps, assume you were never visible to the AI engines at all — most businesses aren't: 9 in 10 websites carry no AI instructions whatsoever.

That's the real diagnosis this question deserves: not "how do I get back on Google," but "can machines read, verify, and recommend my business — anywhere?"

FAQ

Why is my business not showing up on Google Maps?

The most common causes: your Google Business Profile is unverified, suspended, or was never created; your business information (name, address, phone) disagrees across the web, which makes Google distrust the listing; or you're being outranked in your area — Google's own documentation says local ranking comes down to three factors: relevance, distance, and prominence. Start by checking your profile status at google.com/business, then verify your information matches everywhere.

Why did my business stop showing up on Google after verification?

New and re-verified profiles commonly enter a review period, and listings can be filtered if Google detects inconsistent details, a category mismatch, or policy issues. Confirm the profile shows 'verified' status, that your primary category is exactly right, and that your website and profiles agree on name, address, and phone. If the profile was suspended, Google provides a reinstatement request process.

Why is my business not showing up on Google anymore when it used to?

Something changed: a competitor strengthened their profile, your reviews went quiet (local studies find rankings dip after roughly three weeks without fresh reviews), your website became unreadable to crawlers after a redesign, or a suspension or edit affected the listing. Compare your profile against the top-ranked competitor's and check your website's machine-readability — both are checkable free.

If my business doesn't show on Google, is it invisible to ChatGPT too?

Very likely — AI engines read the same signals. One scan checks both worlds: what AI visibility is and how to check yours.
