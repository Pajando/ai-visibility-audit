FAQ

What is AEO in marketing?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing content to be chosen as the direct answer by search engines and AI assistants, instead of just ranking in a list of links. Marketer Jason Barnard says he coined the term in 2017, as Google's featured snippets and direct answers began pushing past the ten blue links.
