AEO has a real history — respect it first

In 2017, as Google's featured snippets and direct answers started pushing past the traditional ten blue links, Jason Barnard named the shift: optimizing not for ranking, but for being chosen as the answer. The term was picked up by the wider SEO industry, documented publicly the following year in Search Engine Watch, and has since become a standard part of SEO-industry conversation. That's a real discipline with a real origin — not a made-up buzzword, and not something we invented to sound similar to it.

FAQ

What is AEO in marketing?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing content to be chosen as the direct answer by search engines and AI assistants, instead of just ranking in a list of links. The term was coined by digital marketer Jason Barnard in 2017, as Google's featured snippets and direct answers began displacing the traditional ten blue links.

Is AEO the same thing as AO?

No, though the names look similar. AEO is a broad industry discipline — a category of tactics, used inconsistently across many agencies with no single fixed checklist. AO (Answer Optimization) is the specific plain-English name for the goal itself: being the answer AI gives your customer.
